PHYNO (EZEGE 1) • PRESS & BIO

Chibuzor Nelson Azubuike (born October 9, 1986), better known as Phyno, is a Nigerian rapper, singer, songwriter, record producer, and founder of Penthauze Music. Renowned for rapping primarily in the Igbo language blended with Nigerian Pidgin and English, Phyno transformed indigenous rap into a mainstream continental movement across Africa.
